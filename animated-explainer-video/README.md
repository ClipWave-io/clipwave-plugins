# Animated Explainer Video Maker: Motion Graphics, No Presenter

Turn a topic into an animated explainer of about 30 seconds: illustrated scenes with motion and a voiceover, no presenter on camera, returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Describe what the video explains and the key points, who it is for and the visual style. Claude prepares the request and shows the credits required. When you confirm, ManyMotions writes the creative brief, scripts four scenes, generates the voiceover, the illustrated frames and their animation, and merges them into one video.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New animated videos from text only, with no presenter on camera: no uploaded footage, photos or real people. Original visuals only: no copyrighted characters or brand logos.

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/animated-explainer-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- An explainer takes several minutes: Claude reports the progress, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_animated_explainer` | Prepare the explainer: topic, audience, style. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_result` | Start the video. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_result` | Report progress, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "Explain how compound interest works for teenagers, flat 2D, playful"
- "A 30-second explainer of how our app splits bills between roommates, clean corporate style"
- "How vaccines train the immune system, isometric illustrations, calm tone"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
