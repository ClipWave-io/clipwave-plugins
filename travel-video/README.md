# Travel Video & Documentary Maker

Turn a destination or a journey into a narrated travel guide, journey diary or short documentary, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Describe the destination or the journey, the angle (travel guide, first-person journey diary or short documentary), the highlights to include and the audience, plus language, format and length. Claude prepares the brief and shows the scenes and the credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Video Agent writes the narration scene by scene, generates the keyframes and the scenes of the place and edits the final cut.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New videos from text only: no uploaded footage or photos. The scenes are generated by AI and illustrate the place; they are not real footage. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/travel-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- A video takes several minutes: Claude reports the steps and the script, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_travel_video` | Prepare the brief: destination, angle, highlights, audience, language, format, length. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_video` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video` | Report progress and the script, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A 30-second travel guide to Kyoto in autumn: temples, maple leaves and a tea house, 16:9"
- "A journey diary of a road trip through Patagonia, first person, 45 seconds, 9:16"
- "A short documentary about the history of the Amalfi coast lemon terraces, narrated in English"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
