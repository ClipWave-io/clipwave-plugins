# Short Film Maker: AI Short Films with Story & Dialogue

Turn a logline into a narrative short film with characters and dialogue, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Give Claude the logline, the genre, the main characters, the setting and how it should end, plus language, format and length. Claude prepares the brief and shows the scenes and the credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Video Agent writes the script scene by scene with one turn and an ending that lands, generates the keyframes and the scenes with a deliberate film look and the dialogue, and edits the final cut.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New films from text only: no uploaded footage, no real actors. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/short-film-maker/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- A video takes several minutes: Claude reports the steps and the script, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_short_film` | Prepare the brief: logline, genre, characters, setting, ending, language, format, length. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_video` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video` | Report progress and the script, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A sci-fi short: a night-shift astronomer receives a message from her future self, 45 seconds, 16:9"
- "A romance short film about two strangers who keep missing the same last train, bittersweet ending"
- "A mystery short set in a 1950s hotel where the elevator stops at a floor that does not exist"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
