# Logo Maker AI & Designer

Design logo concepts from a brand brief in an interactive panel, then render up to four PNG concepts in your ManyMotions account.

## What it does

Describe a brand (name, industry, style, colors). Claude prepares the logo brief in an interactive Logo Maker panel where you adjust model, aspect ratio, resolution and the number of concepts, and shows the credits required. When you confirm, it renders up to four logo concepts with an AI image model in your ManyMotions account and returns the image links. Open any concept in the ManyMotions editor to keep iterating.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

Outputs are raster PNG images. No vector export, no editing of uploaded logos, no trademark checks.

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/logomaker/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- Claude shows an interactive panel where the client supports MCP Apps; Claude Code works with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_logo_project` | Prepare a logo brief and show the panel. Free, no sign-in. |
| `render_logo_widget` | Reopen a prepared project in the panel. |
| `generate_logo` | Render the concepts. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_logo_job` | Check a render and return the image links. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "Design a logo for Northline, an outdoor clothing brand: minimalist, forest green and white"
- "Create 4 logo concepts for a coffee shop called Ember & Oak, warm and rustic"
- "I need a modern logo for a fintech app named Ledgerly, blue tones, geometric"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
