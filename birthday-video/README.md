# Birthday Video Maker: AI Birthday Tribute & Wishes Video

Turn the memories you describe into a personal birthday, anniversary or milestone tribute video, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Tell Claude who the video is for, the occasion, who they are to you, a few real memories (shared moments, habits, phrases they say) and the closing wish, plus language, format and length. Claude prepares the brief and shows the scenes and the credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Video Agent writes the tribute scene by scene, generates memory scenes with voice-over and a closing wish spoken to camera, and edits the final cut.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New videos from text only: the people in the video are generated from your description, photos of the real person cannot be used. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/birthday-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- A video takes several minutes: Claude reports the steps and the script, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_birthday_video` | Prepare the brief: name, occasion, age, relationship, memories, wish, sender, language, format, length. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_video` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video` | Report progress and the script, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "Make a 30-second 90th birthday tribute for Nonna Lucia: Sunday ragù, her garden, the way she says \"mangia!\". Wish: happy 90th, from Marco and the kids"
- "A 15-second anniversary video for my wife Sara, our first trip to Lisbon and the yellow tram, ending with \"ten years and counting\""
- "A retirement tribute for our team lead Paul after 25 years, warm and a little funny, 45 seconds, 16:9"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
