# AI Avatar Presenter & Explainer Video Maker

Turn a topic into a short explainer, lesson or self-introduction presented by an AI avatar with an AI voice, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Describe what the video explains or introduces, who it is for, the points it must cover and how the presenter should look, plus language, format and length. Claude prepares the brief and shows the scenes and the credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Video Agent writes the script scene by scene, generates the keyframes and the scene videos with the AI presenter and voice, and edits the final cut. It is not an ad: no product pitch and no call to action.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New videos from text only: no uploaded footage, no photos of real people, no dubbing. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/ai-avatar-presenter/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- A video takes several minutes: Claude reports the steps and the script, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_presenter_video` | Prepare the brief: topic, explainer or introduction, audience, key points, presenter, language, format, length. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_video` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video` | Report progress and the script, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "Make a 30-second explainer on how compound interest works for high-school students, friendly teacher presenter"
- "Create a 15-second video introducing our family bakery in Lisbon, presented by a warm baker in her 50s, Portuguese"
- "Explain the three types of rock for a geography lesson, 45 seconds, 16:9"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
