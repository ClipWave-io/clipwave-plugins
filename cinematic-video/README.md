# Cinematic AI Video Maker with Camera Moves

Turn a scene description into a cinematic AI video shot with a named camera move, rendered in your ManyMotions account.

## What it does

Describe the scene and pick one camera move from the Cinema Studio catalog of 61 moves (dolly in and out, crane up, 360 orbit, crash zoom, dolly zoom, FPV drone, bullet time, whip pan, hyperlapse and more), plus shot size, lighting, look, format, length and a cinema video model (Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0, Wan 2.7). Claude prepares the shot and shows the settings and the credits required in the ManyMotions panel. When you confirm, ManyMotions renders the clip and Claude returns the video link.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

Text-to-video only: no image-to-video and no editing of uploaded footage. Credits depend on the model and the length and are shown before rendering.

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/cinematic-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- Claude shows an interactive panel with the settings, the credits and the result where the client supports MCP Apps; Claude Code works with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_cinematic_video` | Prepare the shot: scene, camera move, shot, lighting, look, format, length, model. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_media_forge` | Render the prepared shot. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_media_forge_job` | Report progress and return the video link. Read-only. |
| `render_media_forge_widget` | Reopen the panel of a prepared shot. |

## Example prompts

- "A lighthouse keeper climbs the spiral stairs during a storm, crane up, low angle, lightning flashes, 35mm film grain"
- "Slow dolly in on a chef plating a dessert in a dark restaurant kitchen, warm tungsten light, 9:16"
- "FPV drone shot weaving through a neon Tokyo alley at night in the rain, teal and orange look, 10 seconds"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
