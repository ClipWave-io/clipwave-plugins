# Anime Story Video Maker: AI Cartoon & Animated Shorts

Turn a story idea into an animated short with recurring characters, dialogue and narration, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Describe the story, the main characters, the art style (soft anime, flat cel, chibi, cartoon, storybook or 3D animation) and the mood, plus language, format and length. Claude prepares the brief and shows the scenes and the credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Video Agent writes the script scene by scene, generates keyframes and animated scenes in one style held across the whole short, adds the voices and edits the final cut.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New videos from text only: no uploaded footage or reference drawings. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/anime-story-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- A video takes several minutes: Claude reports the steps and the script, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_anime_story` | Prepare the brief: story, characters, art style, mood, language, format, length. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_video` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video` | Report progress and the script, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "An anime short about a paper crane that wakes up in a library and searches for the girl who folded it, heartwarming, 30 seconds"
- "A chibi cartoon where two cats argue over the last sunny spot on the windowsill, funny, 15 seconds, 9:16"
- "A storybook-style tale of a lighthouse that learns to sing, bittersweet, narrated in Spanish"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
