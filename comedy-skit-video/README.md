# Comedy Skit Video Maker: AI Funny Shorts & Parodies

Turn a premise into a short comedy skit with original characters, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Give Claude the premise of the joke, the setting, the characters and the comedy style (deadpan, absurd, parody, observational or slapstick), plus language, format and length. Claude prepares the brief and shows the scenes and the credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Video Agent writes the skit with a fast setup and a punchline, generates the scenes with the dialogue and edits the final cut.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

Original characters only: no real people or public figures, no uploaded footage. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/comedy-skit-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- A video takes several minutes: Claude reports the steps and the script, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_comedy_skit` | Prepare the brief: premise, setting, characters, style, language, format, length. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_video` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video` | Report progress and the script, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A deadpan skit: a man treats the office printer like a moody cat, 15 seconds, 9:16"
- "An absurd skit where a supermarket self-checkout starts giving life advice"
- "A nature-documentary parody about a teenager waking up before noon, narrated in Spanish"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
