# Horror Story Video Maker: AI Scary Shorts

Turn a scary story idea into a narrated horror or suspense short for faceless channels, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Describe the story idea, the setting and the kind of horror (psychological, paranormal, creature, folklore, found footage or thriller), plus language, format and length. Claude prepares the brief and shows the scenes and the credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Video Agent writes the script scene by scene with a slow build and a final break, generates the keyframes and the scenes, adds the narration and edits the final cut.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New videos from text only: no uploaded footage and no real people. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/horror-story-video/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- A video takes several minutes: Claude reports the steps and the script, then returns the MP4 link. Claude Code works the same way, with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_horror_story` | Prepare the brief: premise, setting, kind of horror, language, format, length. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_video` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_video` | Report progress and the script, then return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "A night nurse hears a call bell ringing from a ward that was closed years ago, psychological horror, 30 seconds, 9:16"
- "A found-footage short of hikers who find a cabin with their own photos on the wall, 45 seconds"
- "A folklore horror story about the lantern lights in the marsh, narrated in Portuguese"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
