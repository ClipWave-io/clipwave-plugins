# UGC Video Ads Maker AI

Turn a product brief into a short UGC-style video ad with an AI presenter and voice, produced in your ManyMotions account and returned as an MP4.

## What it does

Describe the product and the audience, and optionally language, tone, format, duration and style. Claude prepares the ad brief and shows the scenes and credits required. When you confirm, the ManyMotions Marketing Agent produces the ad in your account: strategy, script, keyframes, scenes with an AI presenter and an AI voice, and a final cut. The run takes several minutes; Claude reports progress and returns the MP4 link.

**AI generation:** the media are generated by AI models run by ManyMotions and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New AI video ads only. No editing of uploaded footage and no real human creators. 250 credits per scene (8 s = 2 scenes, 15 s = 3, 30 s = 5, 45 s = 7, 60 s = 8).

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the ManyMotions MCP server at `https://manymotions.com/api/claude/ugc/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to ManyMotions (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering needs verified paid access and available credits on the account.
- Claude shows an interactive panel where the client supports MCP Apps; Claude Code works with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_ugc_video_ad` | Prepare the ad brief: scenes and credits. Free, no sign-in. |
| `generate_ugc_video_ad` | Start the production. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_ugc_video_ad` | Report progress and return the MP4 link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "Create a 15-second TikTok ad for my skincare serum for women 25-45 in the US"
- "Make a UGC testimonial video ad for a productivity app for remote workers, 9:16, energetic"
- "Generate a 30-second product demo ad for a stainless steel water bottle, friendly tone"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to ManyMotions and stored in your ManyMotions account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
